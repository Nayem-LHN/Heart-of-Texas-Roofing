Is it time for a new roof?

Most asphalt roofs in Central Texas last 15–20 years due to extreme heat stress. Look for these warning signs:

  • Curling or missing shingles
  • Granules collecting in gutters
  • Visible leaks or ceiling stains
  • Impact damage from hail storms
